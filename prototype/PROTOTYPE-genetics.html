Genetic Game of Life PROTOTYPE, throwaway

Question this answers: does the Mendelian genetics model look and feel right on top of Conway's Life? Every live Organism carries two Pigment Alleles. The stronger Allele decides its color, and equal ones blend. At each Birth, two random live Neighbours are the Parents, and each passes on one of its two Alleles at random. Births and deaths follow the Rule exactly, so genetics never changes who lives, only what they carry. Try the guided scenarios below, or play freely.
